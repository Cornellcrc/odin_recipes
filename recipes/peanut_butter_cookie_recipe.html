Peanut Butter Cookies

Description

A recipe for soft, thick peanut butter cookies that can yield about 32 cookies.

  • Prep time: Approx. 1 hour, 30 minutes (including chilling)
  • Cook time: Approx. 12 minutes
  • Total time: Approx 1 hour, 45 minutes

Tools/Equipment

  • Medium Bowl
  • Large bowl
  • Whisk
  • Rubber Spatula or Bowl scraper
  • Mixer (Handheld or Standing)
  • Measuring spoons
    • 1 cup
    • 1/2 cup
    • 1/3 cup
    • 3/4 cup
    • 1/4 cup
    • 1 tablespoon
    • 1 teaspoon
    • 1/2 teaspoon
    • 1/4 teaspoon
  • Baking Sheets
  • Parchment Paper or A silcone Baking Mat

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/3 cups of All-purpose Flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon of Baking Soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon of Salt
  • 1/2 cup of Salted or unsalted Butter, softend to room temp
  • 1/2 cup of light or dark Brown Sugar
  • 1/4 cup of Granulated Sugar for the actual dough
    • -Optional - 1/2 cup for rolling the prepared dough in as a coating
  • 1 Large Egg at room temp
  • 3/4 cup of Creamy or Smooth Peanut Butter
  • 1 teaspoon of Pure Vanilla Extract

Instructions

  1. First, in the medium bowl, whisk the flour, baking soda and salt together, and then just set aside.
  2. In the large bowl, using the mixer (standing or hand), beat the butter on high until creamy.
  3. Next switch to medium-high, and beat in the brown sugar and granulated sugar, until completely creamy and smooth.
  4. After that, add egg and beat until combined. Ensure you use the spatula or bowl scraper to scrape the sides and bottom so it's fully mixed.
  5. Then add the peanut butter and vanilla extract, and beat until combined.
  6. Next add the dry ingredients from the medium bowl into the wet ingredients in the large bowl, and beat until fully combined, when the dough is creamy and soft.
  7. When the dough is fully combined, cover the dough tightly with a tight container or saran wrap, and refrigerate it for at least 1-2 hours, and up to 3 days.
  8. Preheat the oven to 350 F (177 C), and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or silicone baking mats, sprayed with a bit of cooking spray and/or lightly dusted with flour.
  9. Take the dough out of the refridgerator and let chill for about 5 minutes. Then depending on your preferences, you can...
    • With Coating and decoration: Place the remaining 1/2 cup of granulated sugar into a bowl, take a piece of the cookie dough (about a tablespoon), roll it into a ball, roll it into the sugar, shape it, press to flatten slightly, and arragnge on the baking sheet. Repeat with the rest of the dough. You can also use a fork to make a criss-cross indent on top of each.
    • Without Coating: Simply take a piece of the cookie dough, roll it into a ball, shape it/press to flatten slightly, and arrange them on the baking sheet. Repeat with the rest of the dough.

    - TIP - Remember the cookies will expand and grow as they bake, so make sure there is enough space between the cookies and that they will be your ideal size when fully baked.

    - TIP - when making the individual cookies by hand, remember in addition to proper sanitation, you should continously dust your hands with flour to avoid the dough from sticking too much to your hands.

  10. Finally Bake for about 11-12 minutes or until until the edges and bottom are set and lightly browned, ideally with a soft center
  11. When fully baked, take them out and allow the cookies to cool for about 10 minutes.
  12. Once cooled, you can either enjoy immediately or store them for later. If sealed tightly, they can last for about a week or 10 days at room temp, about two weeks refridgerated, or about 3 months when freezed.
