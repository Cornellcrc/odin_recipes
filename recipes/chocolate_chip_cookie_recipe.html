Description

A recipe for soft chew chocolate chip cookies that can yield about 15 to 24 cookies, depending on the size.

  • Prep time: Approx. 15 minutes
  • Cook time: Appox. 13 minutes
  • Total time: Approx. 2 hours 45 minutes

Tools/Equipment

  • Medium Bowl
  • Large bowl
  • Whisk
  • Rubber Spatula or Bowl scraper
  • Wooden spoon or Rubber Spatula
  • Measuring spoons
    • 1 Cup
    • 3/4 Cup
    • 1/4 Cup
    • 1 tablespoon
    • 1 teaspoon
    • 1/2 teaspoon
    • 1/4 teaspoon
  • Baking Sheets
  • Parchment Paper or A silcone Baking Mat
  • -Optional Mixer (Handheld or Standing)

Ingredients

  • 2 and 1/4 cups of All-purpose Flour
  • 1 teaspoon of Baking Soda
  • 1 and 1/2 teaspoons of Cornstarch
  • 1/2 teaspoon of Salt
  • 3/4 cup of Unsalted Butter, melted and cooled, or at room temp
  • 3/4 cup of Light or Dark Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 cup of Granulated Sugar
  • 1 Large Egg and 1 Egg yolk at room temp
  • 2 teaspoons of Pure Vanilla Extract
  • 1 and 1/4 cups of semi-sweet chocolate chips or chocolate chunks

Instructions

  1. In the large bowl, combine and whisk the flour, baking soda and salt together, then set aside.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk, and/or use a mixer on medium, handheld or standing, the butter, brown sugar and granulated sugar together until combined.
  3. Next add the egg and egg yolk into the medium bowl, and whisk until combined. Then add vanilla extract and whisk again. Ideally, the mixture should look thin.
  4. Then pour the wet ingredients in the medium bowl into the dry ingredients in the large bowl, and proceed to mix with either a large spoon/spatula for more soft, thick and shiny texture, or simply a mixer.
  5. Now add the chocolate chips and mix with a large spoon -TIP - try your best to mix the chocolate chip into the dough as it may not stick if you use melted butter.
  6. Cover the dough tightly and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or up to 3 days.
  7. Preheat the oven to 325 F (163 C) and line the baking sheets with parchment paper or silcone baking mats, and spray with cooking spray. Also take the dough out of the refridgerator (if the dough has been chilled for longer than 2 hours, let it sit at room temp for about 15 minutes ). Also lightly dust the parchment paper/baking mat and your hands with flour.
  8. Then take pieces of the cookie dough, either by...
    • using a cookie scoop or tablespoon to scoop the dough
    • or by hand
    ,and proceed to roll, shape, press and put the dough on the baking sheet. Repeat with remaining dough.

    - TIP - Remember the cookies will expand and grow as they bake, so make sure there is enough space between the cookies and that they will be your ideal size when fully baked.

    - TIP - if you want the cookies to bake thicker, then you might want to use your fingers to shape the cookie to have a greater lenghth than width, similar to a cylinder

    - TIP - when making the individual cookies by hand, remember in addition to proper sanitation, you should continously dust your hands with flour to avoid the dough from sticking too much to your hands.

  9. Bake the cookies for about 13-14 minutes or until edges and bottom are very lightly browned. If the oven has hot spots or you want to be more sure it will bake properly, you can rotate the baking sheet(s) halfway through the time.
  10. WHen finished, take the cookies out and let the cool for about 10 minutes. If you want, you can press in a few more chocolate chips while warm.
  11. Once cooled, you can either enjoy or store them for later. If stored tightly, they should last for about a week to 10 days at room temp, about two weeks refridgerated or about 3 months if freezed.
